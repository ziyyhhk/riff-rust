**The big one for your friends' reports.**

- Language selection actually works now: the `/api/i18n-batch` endpoint behind Settings → Interface → Language is implemented (DeepL if you add your own key under Advanced, otherwise keyless Google Translate with automatic fallback to MyMemory). Previously switching languages silently reverted to English.
- Icons are bundled locally (Material Symbols shipped inside the app now, instead of Google Fonts). When the CDN was unreachable, icons rendered as overflowing text that blocked clicks and broke the sidebar/settings layout.
- Unchanged but included: album/Daily Mix tracks resolve through YouTube Music search, lyrics visible (cover-mode reset), 12.5 Hz highlight throttle, Discord RPC non-blocking pipe, instant local-track playback, SoundCloud HLS.
- If something still misbehaves on a friend's PC after this update, capture the exact clicks and the last lines of `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Riff Rust\logs\riff-rust.log`.

Old releases are being removed; this is the only release.
