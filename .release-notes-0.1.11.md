**The "Track can't be loaded" fix, plus album tracks that actually play.**

- Daily Mix / album rows with non-YouTube ids (`alb_dm_...`, Deezer-sourced tracks) are now resolved through YouTube Music search by artist/title. Previously every such row was sent to YouTube as `watch?v=alb_...`, failed, and could be replaced by different music.
- Failed tracks are no longer ripped out of the loaded album/mix while you're in it; the row stays and playback retries twice before any error is shown.
- Stale failed-track history from the broken version is cleared once, so your Daily Mixes are complete again.
- SoundCloud tracks that only expose HLS (newer uploads) now play directly through hls.js in ~1 s instead of falling into a slow yt-dlp timeout.
- SoundCloud pre-fetching no longer spawns yt-dlp for every queued row.

YouTube playback itself is unchanged (VISIONOS full-range streams, ~0.3–2 s first play).
