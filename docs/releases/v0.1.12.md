**Why songs felt stuck for 10–60 s — fixed for real this time.**

- Root cause (measured on this PC): the background MP3 cache went through yt-dlp's whole-file download, which runs ~10–60 s per song. New songs could never play until that finished, and the WebView then tried an AAC/m4a it can't decode (WebView2 on this machine only decodes mp3/wav/flac) → silent "code=4" → the long recovery path. That's the "song takes forever" loop.
- The cache now pulls the fresh VISIONOS URL in **1 MB Range chunks** (~1–2 s for a full track; whole-file GETs are throttled, but Range requests run at full speed) and transcodes locally. `/api/stream-info` uses the same pipeline, and prefetch resolves its URL up front.
- Discord Rich Presence: the reader thread was holding the pipe mutex during a blocking read, so every `SET_ACTIVITY` starved. The pipe is now two handles (read/write), Discord gets your presence, and the app logs "discord activity sent".
- Still includes: synthetic album/Daily Mix ids resolved via YouTube Music search, failures no longer replace album rows, stale failed-track cache cleared on upgrade, SoundCloud HLS direct playback.

Tip: on this machine the first-ever play of a song pays a ~2–5 s one-time transcode; every play after that is instant. Fully-closed queue prefetch keeps subsequent songs instant too.
